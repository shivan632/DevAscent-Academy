## Brand & Style

The visual narrative is engineered around disciplined credibility, modern technical rigor, and conversion momentum. Built for ambitious Indian engineers and career-switchers, the experience balances the institutional confidence of a Tier-1 tech institute with the agility and high visual impact of modern Silicon Valley software tooling. 

The aesthetic aligns with modern corporate-technical design:
- **Tone:** Methodical, aspirational, transparent, and authoritative.
- **Visual Stance:** Pristine structural layout, crisp structural divider lines, subtle directional gradients in focal sections, and stark signal accents designed to highlight outcome metrics (curriculum depth, placement stats, and checkout trust signals).
- **Core Narrative:** Eliminate career skepticism through immediate visual clarity—relying on high-contrast numeric data, transparent curriculum trees, verified certification credentials, and streamlined transactional confidence.

## Colors

The color palette coordinates electric indigo energy with deep slate structure and accreditation emerald:

- **Primary (`#4F46E5` / `#6366F1`):** Represents dynamic execution. Reserved strictly for primary call-to-action triggers, active progress states, interactive code module selections, and key conversion nodes.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** Outcome-oriented emerald green. Designated exclusively for proof of achievement, verified certification badges, career placement metrics, module completion states, and secure Razorpay payment confirmations.
- **Tertiary (`#0B0F19` / `#111827`):** Slate dark anchors. Used for terminal UI previews, top-level navigation bars, deep-contrast feature callouts, and authoritative typography headers.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - Base background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle alternating sections in Slate White (`#F8FAFC`).
  - Structural lines & card borders: Crisp Slate Gray (`#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`).
  - Text: Primary Slate Dark (`#0F172A`) ensures WCAG AAA contrast, supported by Secondary Slate (`#475569`) for metadata and subtitles.

## Typography

The pairing of **Plus Jakarta Sans** and **Inter** resolves the dichotomy between high-converting marketing presence and precise technical legibility:

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans. Its geometric apertures and contemporary posture convey forward momentum. Hero banners and pricing blocks utilize tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for punchy visual density.
- **Body & Technical Specs:** Set in Inter. Chosen for its neutral, highly standardized metrics, open counters, and exceptional clarity when displaying monospace code snippets, bullet points, and syllabus breakdowns.
- **Numbers & Currencies:** Indian Rupee symbols (`₹`) and numerical cohort values inherit font metrics seamlessly; tabular figures are preferred for syllabus timetables and pricing tables to align decimal values and dates cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in an 8pt architectural grid mapped across a responsive 12-column framework:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** Max-width canvas capped at 1200px. Standard 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) outer margins. Complex sections (such as course syllabus vs. cohort progress) use an 8-column content / 4-column sticky conversion card pattern.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters. Secondary sidebars reflow beneath primary lesson tracks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px margins. Sticky bottom bar locks onto screen viewport for immediate cohort enrollment actions.
- **Vertical Cadence:** Strict separation between major narrative sections is anchored at 64px (`4rem`), scaling down to 40px (`2.5rem`) on mobile to maintain reading cadence without excessive dead space.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of a layered "technical surface" model that highlights crisp boundaries:

- **Surface Levels:**
  - `Level 0 (Base Canvas)`: `#FFFFFF` for primary content modules, `#F8FAFC` for secondary bands (testimonials, FAQ, verified outcomes).
  - `Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)`: Background `#FFFFFF` encased in a crisp 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). A light ambient shadow is applied: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
  - `Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Sticky Bars)`: Sticky header, bottom mobile action bar, and syllabus preview modals receive elevated ambient layering: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` combined with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`).
  - `Level 3 (Dark Technical Callouts)`: Deep `#0B0F19` background surfaces with zero shadow, framed by a delicate indigo-tinted border (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2)`), offering an IDE/terminal-like contrast for hands-on project showcases.

## Shapes

With a roundedness factor of `2`, the system enforces calibrated geometry:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** 8px (`0.5rem`) border-radius (`rounded`). Provides a welcoming, modern feel without sacrificing structural precision.
- **Containers & Cards:** 16px (`1rem`) border-radius (`rounded-lg`). Provides balanced contouring around multi-line technical modules and curriculum modules.
- **Hero Highlights & Modals:** 24px (`1.5rem`) border-radius (`rounded-xl`). Reserved for standout checkout surfaces, guarantee cards, and hero media containers.
- **Micro Tags & Trust Badges:** Fully rounded pill radius (`9999px`) for security stamps, verified indicators, and status tags to visually distinguish them from rectangular layout blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Enroll / Checkout):** Solid `#4F46E5` background, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px radius, height 48px (desktop) / 44px (mobile), font `label-md`. Subtle hover transition to `#4338CA` with an elevation state change to Level 2.
- **Secondary / Outline (Syllabus Download):** Transparent background, 1px solid border `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover background shifts to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Guarantee / Trust CTA:** Primary button paired with inline trust micro-copy: "Instant access • Razorpay secured • 7-day money-back guarantee".

### Badges & Trust Seals
- **Verified Accreditation Badge:** Pill-shaped, background `#ECFDF5`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`, paired with an emerald green checkmark icon.
- **Razorpay Verified Seal:** Slate border `#E2E8F0` with official Razorpay badge integration, guaranteeing UPI, EMI, and card support.
- **Live Cohort Status:** Background `#EEF2FF`, border `1px solid #C7D2FE`, text `#3730A3`, leading with a pulsing status dot.

### Form Inputs & Checkout Fields
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, border-radius 8px, background `#FFFFFF`, font `body-md`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#4F46E5` with a 3px ring of `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`. No blurry glows; clear boundary focus for speed and accessibility.
- **Validation Messages:** Red `#EF4444` for invalid phone/OTP inputs; Emerald `#10B981` for coupon applied states.

### Course & Module Cards
- **Syllabus Accordion / Card:** White card with 1px `#E2E8F0` outline, 16px radius, internal padding of 24px (`1.5rem`). Header row displays module duration, live lecture count, and expandable project tags.
- **Project Detail Box:** `#0B0F19` dark terminal surface nested within lesson modules, featuring syntax-accented directory structures and real-world repository previews.

### Pricing Cards
- **Flagship Cohort Card:** Highlighted with a 2px top accent line in `#4F46E5`. Includes explicit `₹ INR` pricing with crossed-out early-bird anchors, EMI breakdown labels (e.g., "Starting at ₹2,499/mo"), and prominent seat countdown chips.
